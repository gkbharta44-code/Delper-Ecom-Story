SBLC Bridge — Strategic Context

The SBLC Bridge is Delper's contingency capital instrument, activated only if any FPO round is undersubscribed. Under base-case projections, all 2,200 cities are fully funded through trade programme profits ($401.44T), IPO/FPO equity ($135T), and the DPR token pledge loan ($137.31T). The SBLC is the guarantee that the programme always executes regardless. A callable Standby Letter of Credit (MT760 SWIFT, first-demand, unconditional) issued by a minimum BBB+/Baa1-rated bank backs each city's construction loan. On practical completion, the SBLC is replaced by the city asset itself ($306.25B valuation) and city rental revenue ($21.44B/city/yr) services the debt — eliminating any ongoing government guarantee dependency.

1
BREACH CLASSIFICATION RECEIVED — ACRE receives a confirmed breach classification from Agent 5 or ASHE with: breach class (BC-1 to BC-6), origin wallet(s) or block range, breach timestamp, and the pre-computed transaction graph from the D2 Grover index. The annealer traces every outbound transfer from every tainted wallet, then every outbound transfer from those recipients, recursively, with no depth limit. Graph expansion completes in <2 seconds for up to 10,000 wallet hops. Time: T+2s.
3
SIMULTANEOUS FREEZE — ALL TAINTED WALLETS — ACRE issues a NetworkFreeze transaction to the Hyperledger settlement contract listing all tainted wallets from the graph traversal. The contract freezes all listed wallets simultaneously in a single atomic transaction — no wallet in the list can execute any outbound transfer from this point forward. 4
TAINTED AMOUNT CALCULATION PER WALLET — For each frozen wallet, ACRE calculates the exact tainted token amount: the number of DPR tokens in that wallet that can be traced back to the breach origin via the transaction graph. Wallets may hold both tainted and legitimate tokens. ACRE calculates only the tainted portion — legitimate holdings are not frozen beyond what is needed for the pullback. Time: T+4s (quantum-accelerated parallel calculation across all wallets simultaneously).
5
REVERSE CHRONOLOGICAL PULLBACK EXECUTION — ACRE issues PullbackOrder transactions in reverse chronological order: the most recent tainted transfers are recalled first (these have the least finality and are most recoverable), working back to the origin. Each PullbackOrder transfers the calculated tainted amount from the destination wallet back to a dedicated ACRE Recovery Vault — a Hyperledger escrow address controlled jointly by CISO + CFO multi-sig. Tokens accumulate in the Recovery Vault until the sweep is complete. Time: T+5s to T+10s depending on graph size.
6
SWEEP COMPLETION VERIFICATION — ACRE verifies that the total tokens recovered into the Recovery Vault match the total tainted amount calculated in step 4. If the totals match: sweep confirmed complete, all frozen wallets unfrozen (except the origin breach wallet), and the Recovery Vault balance is reported to CISO + CFO. If the totals do not match (tokens moved between graph expansion and freeze): ACRE re-runs the graph traversal from the frozen state and issues supplementary pullbacks for any remaining tainted amounts. Time: T+10s to T+15s for verification.
7
RECOVERY VAULT DISPOSITION — Tokens in the Recovery Vault are held in multi-sig escrow (CISO + CFO) until post-breach review is complete. Legitimate recipients who received tokens from the breach unknowingly (e.g. a merchant who accepted a payment later found to originate from a hacked wallet) are identified during CISO review.
